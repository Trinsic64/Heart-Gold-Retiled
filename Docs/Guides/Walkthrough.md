# Expansion Walkthrough

This walkthrough focuses on the altered progression, boss teams, Mega Evolution, and offline events in **HeartGold Retiled Expansion v6.10.0**. The map/story route remains recognizable as Pokémon HeartGold, but wild encounters and trainer parties should be treated as new.

## Before starting

1. Apply `HG-Retiled-Expansion-Patch.xdelta` directly to the compatible clean Pokémon HeartGold (USA) ROM listed in the repository README. Do not stack it on the visual-only patch.
2. Keep the clean ROM and your save backups separate. Never overwrite your only save.
3. Use the [Wild Encounter Guide](Wild-Encounter-Guide.md) to plan captures, the [Evolution Guide](Evolution-Guide.md) for non-vanilla methods, and the [Item Location Guide](Item-Locations.md) for stones and pickups.

## 1. New Bark Town to Violet City

- Follow Elm's opening errand through Cherrygrove to Mr. Pokémon's house.
- Professor Oak gives the **Mega Ring immediately after the Pokédex**. From this point, Mega Evolution is enabled when a compatible Pokémon holds its stone.
- Route 29 already has a heavily expanded roster that changes by time of day. Build a balanced party instead of waiting for the old Johto defaults.
- Clear Sprout Tower, then challenge Falkner.

**Falkner:** Pidgeotto Lv. 14<br>Staravia Lv. 14<br>Trumbeak Lv. 14. His whole team is level 14; Rock, Electric, and Ice coverage is useful, but check for coverage moves rather than relying on one counter.

## 2. Violet City to Azalea Town

- Travel south through Route 32 and Union Cave.
- Resolve Team Rocket's operation in Slowpoke Well, then enter Azalea Gym.

**Bugsy:** Butterfree Lv. 15<br>Beautifly Lv. 15<br>Dustox Lv. 15<br>Vivillon Lv. 15<br>Beedrill Lv. 17. Status powders and Bug/Flying coverage are a bigger threat than in vanilla. The ace Beedrill holds Beedrillite, so bring reliable Rock, Fire, Flying, or Psychic damage and avoid letting status snowball.

## 3. Ilex Forest and Goldenrod City

- Defeat Silver, complete the Farfetch'd puzzle, and cross Ilex Forest.
- In Goldenrod, obtain the Bicycle and Radio Card, then prepare for Whitney.

**Whitney:** Furret Lv. 18<br>Wigglytuff Lv. 18<br>Miltank Lv. 19<br>Audino Lv. 20. Her four-Pokémon Normal/Fairy-leaning team has more coverage than the vanilla Miltank fight. Fighting moves help, but do not assume every target shares Normal's weakness.

## 4. Ecruteak City

- Clear the Sudowoodo route, visit the Burned Tower, and advance the legendary-beast story.
- Several retained Mega Stone pickups are already available across early Johto. Other stones come from wild-family held items, including eligible prior evolutions.

**Morty:** Haunter Lv. 21<br>Corsola Galarian Lv. 22<br>Mismagius Lv. 23<br>Mimikyu Lv. 24<br>Gengar Lv. 25. Normal-type immunity tricks alone are insufficient because the team includes regional forms and coverage. Dark and Ghost attacks are still strong options.

## 5. Olivine and Cianwood

- Climb the Lighthouse, Surf to Cianwood, collect the SecretPotion, and challenge Chuck.

**Chuck:** Annihilape Lv. 29<br>Falinks Lv. 30<br>Tauros Combat Lv. 30<br>Poliwrath Lv. 31<br>Pawmot Lv. 30<br>Lucario Lv. 32. Expect a six-member Fighting roster with modern threats and mixed secondary typings; Psychic, Flying, and Fairy coverage should be spread across more than one party member.

- Return to Olivine, cure Amphy, and challenge Jasmine.

**Jasmine:** Skarmory Lv. 35<br>Forretress Lv. 34<br>Magnezone Lv. 36<br>Empoleon Lv. 37<br>Aegislash Lv. 37<br>Steelix Lv. 38. Fire, Fighting, and Ground coverage is important, but her six-member Steel team includes answers to simple type sweeps.

## 6. Mahogany, Lake of Rage, and Radio Tower

- Investigate the red Gyarados and clear the Team Rocket HQ.
- Challenge Pryce, then resolve the Goldenrod Radio Tower takeover.

**Pryce:** Ninetales Alolan Lv. 37<br>Weavile Lv. 37<br>Mamoswine Lv. 38<br>Walrein Lv. 38<br>Cetitan Lv. 39<br>Glalie Lv. 40. His full Ice roster now opens with Alolan Ninetales and includes secondary typings that punish a single Fire-only plan. Carry Fighting, Rock, or Steel alternatives.

## 7. Blackthorn and the eighth badge

- Cross Ice Path, challenge Clair, then complete the Dragon's Den test.

**Clair:** Noivern Lv. 40<br>Dragalge Lv. 41<br>Dragonair Lv. 40<br>Gyarados Lv. 42<br>Kingdra Lv. 43<br>Charizard Lv. 45. Her six-member Dragon team sits around levels 40–45. Fairy is enabled in this build and is the cleanest new answer, but Steel and Ice support remain valuable.

## 8. Kimono Girls and the Pokémon League

- Complete the Kimono Girl sequence and the version story encounter, then cross Routes 26–27 and Victory Road.
- The two highlighted Victory Road trainers are Gaven (ID 102) and Blake (ID 103); both carry six-member level-appropriate teams.
- Prepare a broad six-Pokémon roster for the League rather than a narrow Johto-only counter team.

- **Will:** Armarouge Lv. 41<br>Bronzong Lv. 42<br>Slowking Lv. 43<br>Gallade Lv. 44<br>Hatterene Lv. 45<br>Gardevoir Lv. 46
- **Koga:** Naganadel Lv. 43<br>Sneasler Lv. 42<br>Drapion Lv. 43<br>Toxtricity Lv. 44<br>Iron Moth Lv. 45<br>Venusaur Lv. 46
- **Bruno:** Hitmontop Lv. 42<br>Heracross Lv. 43<br>Conkeldurr Lv. 44<br>Hawlucha Lv. 44<br>Annihilape Lv. 45<br>Lucario Lv. 46

## 10. Mt. Silver and Red

- Obtain access to Mt. Silver after all 16 badges and prepare for the strongest primary story battle.
- Red's team is Pikachu Lv. 88<br>Lapras Lv. 80<br>Snorlax Lv. 82<br>Venusaur Lv. 84<br>Charizard Lv. 84<br>Blastoise Lv. 84. The average level is above 83 and three team members hold Mega Stones, so bring a trained, item-equipped team with answers to Electric, Water/Ice, Normal, Grass/Poison, Fire/Flying, and Water coverage.

## 11. Offline event checklist

1. Obtain the National Dex.
2. Visit National Park for the one-time Lv. 30 Shaymin, then take Shaymin to the Goldenrod Flower Shop for Gracidea.
3. See all 28 Unown. This enables the Lv. 50 Arceus at Ruins of Alph.
4. With the National Dex and all 28 Unown, collect the Enigma Stone from the Ruins of Alph Research Center and take it to Pewter Museum.
5. After clearing the Elite Four, return to Ilex Forest shrine for the Lv. 30 Celebi and guaranteed-shiny Lv. 30 Pichu encounters. Use them with the original shrine sequences.
6. Complete the Sinjoh event, then find the Lv. 50 Diancie in Sinjoh Ruins.
7. Complete the original late-game orb scene to receive both Red Orb and Blue Orb and finish all Embedded Tower legendary paths on one save.

## 12. Completion and rematches

- Collect the 79 visible Mega Stones and hunt the held-item sources for the other 13; wild/static sources can also provide alternatives for many retained pickups.
- Challenge the Gym and League rematches listed in the [Trainer Guide](Trainer-Guide.md).
- Keep rotating saves while filling the Items pocket; the release audit calls out an unverified worst-case capacity scenario near 290 distinct item entries.
