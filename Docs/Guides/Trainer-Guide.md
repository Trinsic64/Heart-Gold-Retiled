# Trainer Guide

Trainer parties in HeartGold Retiled Expansion are extensively rebalanced. Regular trainers follow stronger class themes and draw from the expanded species roster; major trainers have curated teams, items, moves, and rematches.

## Practical advice

- Do not plan around vanilla HeartGold teams. Check the boss tables before entering a Gym or the Pokémon League.
- Several bosses carry Mega Stones. Because the Mega Ring is enabled early, prepare for Mega-capable opponents from the early Gyms onward.
- Regular trainers often cover their class's usual weaknesses with newer species or regional forms.
- The complete index is sorted by internal trainer ID because map scripts refer to those IDs. Use browser search for a trainer name.
